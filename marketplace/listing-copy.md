# AlignIQ — Jira Marketplace listing draft

## Tagline

Turn Jira delivery history into credible quarterly commitments across teams.

## Summary

AlignIQ connects selected Jira Software boards into a facilitator-first quarterly planning workspace. Teams keep delivery work in Jira while leaders see dependencies, flow evidence, forecast ranges, and governed outcome objectives without adopting the overhead of a full product-management suite.

## Highlights

- Create and inspect native Jira issue dependencies from Jira and the AlignIQ program board.
- Coordinate multiple team boards while preserving each team’s delivery baseline.
- Generate team and program flow metrics from Jira status history.
- Use probability-based planning ranges after a sufficient historical baseline exists.
- Keep financial assumptions, program intelligence, and objective governance in a leadership-only view.
- Export or delete a client tenant through Super Admin controls.

## What AlignIQ is not

AlignIQ is not a product roadmapping, employee productivity, time-tracking, or investment-advice tool. Story points remain team-relative. Cost per point is an allocation model and must not be used to compare teams or individuals.
