# Pricing hypothesis for validation

AlignIQ should launch as a paid cloud app with a 30-day trial and two editions:

- **Standard:** dependency planning, multi-board synchronization, standup briefs, team flow metrics, and program flow visibility.
- **Advanced:** predictive delivery ranges, governed delivery economics, cited AI value evidence, and quarterly objective history/realized-result comparison.

The initial price should be tested with design partners before submission. A defensible starting hypothesis is approximately **US$2 per Jira user/month for Standard** and **US$3.50 per Jira user/month for Advanced**, with Atlassian tier pricing reducing the effective per-user rate at larger sites. Final pricing must be entered in Atlassian’s vendor console and checked against the current Marketplace revenue share, billing tiers, and edition rules before launch.

The value story is reduced coordination overhead and more credible delivery commitments—not replacing Jira, product management, or financial planning.
